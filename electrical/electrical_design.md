## 1. Scope

The sortation conveyor receives cartons and totes from an upstream infeed. It uses photo-electric sensors to classify each load into one of three destination classes, then diverts it to the matching outbound lane. Each lane transfers the load to an AGV or RGV pickup station, or to an AS/RS infeed conveyor. The sorter does not release a load until downstream equipment signals ready.

The electrical scope covered here is:

- Incoming three-phase supply, main isolator, short-circuit protection, and motor protection.
- Belt drive by inverter (VFD) with analogue speed reference and fault feedback.
- 24 V DC control supply and distribution.
- Emergency stop through a dedicated safety relay driving drive STO and the diverter-solenoid contactor.
- Siemens S7-1200 PLC with ten digital inputs, seven digital outputs, one analogue input, and one analogue output.
- Field devices: pushbuttons, one proximity sensor, four photo-electric sensors, three diverter solenoid valves, beacon, horn, run lamp, and downstream-ready interface.
- A servo-driven transfer unit, retained as a design option (section 7).

A **VFD** (Variable Frequency Drive) varies motor speed. **STO** (Safe Torque Off) is a hardware drive input that prevents motor torque. These specific design elements are for process and design understanding; real electrical/safety work must be verified by qualified engineers.

Output common `3L+` is fed from `-F5`; `3M` returns to 0 V. Every output drives an interposing relay, so the PLC output never carries final-load current.

`Q0.0` and `Q0.6` come from the same run condition. `Q0.0` is the internal belt-run state for the run lamp and spare permissive contact to a neighbouring conveyor. `Q0.6` is the real run command intended for the inverter. Separating them makes the drive interface visible and explicit in the drawing and program.

### 3.3 Analogue I/O

| PLC addr | PLC tag | Module | Terminal | Wire | Device | Range | Description |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| IW64 | `Speed_Setpoint` | `-A1` AI0 | `-X1:20` / `-X1:21` | 401 / 402 | Speed potentiometer `-R1`, 10 kΩ | 0–10 V = 0–27648 | Operator belt-speed setpoint |
| QW80 | `VFD_Speed_Ref` | `-A2` SB 1232 AQ | `-X1:22` / `-X1:23` | 403 / 404 | Inverter `AI1` / `AGND` | 0–10 V = 0–27648 | Belt-speed reference to drive |

An analogue signal carries a value over a range rather than only on/off. Here the PLC’s raw range 0–27648 maps to 0–10 V, allowing the potentiometer setting to become the drive speed reference.
